IN ABSENTIA: Offline Encrypted End-of-Life and Emergency Record

IN ABSENTIA is a browser-local posthumous and emergency dossier builder. Answer nine prompts in volatile memory and export a standalone, self-decrypting HTML file protected by PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000 iterations) and AES-256-GCM encryption with zero servers and zero accounts.

  • I. The Corpse and The Witness: Legal identity and designated executor or witness.
  • II. The Cipher and The Clue: Master passphrase and non-sensitive memory clue.
  • III. The First Forty-Eight Hours: Immediate instructions, burial or cremation wishes, and urgent contacts.
  • IV. Where the Wealth Lies: Bank accounts, brokerages, crypto vaults, and safe locations.
  • V. Blood Money and Policies: Life insurance, health policies, employer death benefits, and claim numbers.
  • VI. Leeches to Sever: Mortgages, rent, utilities, and recurring subscriptions to cancel.
  • VII. The Helpless and The Keys: Guardianship plans, pet care, spare keys, and alarm or safe codes.
  • VIII. Digital Ghosts: Phone unlock hints, password manager emergency access, and Apple or Google legacy contacts.
  • IX. Final Words from the Grave: Personal parting letters addressed to loved ones.
